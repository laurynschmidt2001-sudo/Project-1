## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Mouse Escape: A text based adventure where a tiny house mouse navigates a home to gather items to slip past the resident cat.

**Storyline:**

You are a tiny house mousetrying to escape a cozy home wihtout getting caught by the resident cat Mr. Kitty. Starting from the safety of your Mousehole, you must sneak through the rooms to collect six distraction items: a yarn ball, a laser pointer, a bell, catnip, a scrunhie, and a feather wand. Mr. Kitty guards the Sunroom, and entering before you have all six items means getting caught and losing the game. Once you gather every item, you can use them to distract Mr. Kitty and slip outside to freedom.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Yarn Ball
2. Laser Pointer
3. Bell
4. Catnip
5. Scrunchie
6. Feather Wand

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain
